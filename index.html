How to play

  • Letters fly up. Drag through them to cut them into your word.
  • Lift your finger to submit. Three letters or more.
  • Letters are cut in the order you touch them, so plan the path.
  • Long words pay far more, and five letters or longer buy you time.
  • A wrong word costs three seconds and drops your multiplier.
  • Cut three words in a row for ×2, and keep going for more.
  • 💣 Bombs cost ten seconds and void the word you were cutting. Late in a run that is the end of it.

Missing a letter costs nothing. Let the bad ones fall.

The clock tops out at a minute and a half, however well you cut.
